CARLA was built for structured, right-hand traffic. Gwalior's roads are neither. My M.Tech thesis with Dr. Rahul Kala builds a real-to-sim pipeline that replays real Indian trajectories in CARLA Town10HD, so driving policies can be trained and stress-tested on behavior they would otherwise never see.

Code: [github.com/rajvamsi12/real2sim-trajectory-style-transfer](https://github.com/rajvamsi12/real2sim-trajectory-style-transfer)

## From video to scenarios

- A 6-region calibrated mapping takes left-hand traffic into Town10HD, using size-matched vehicle blueprints so CARLA Autopilot can be benchmarked on the same scenes.
- Out of **26,868** candidates, **5,212** valid scenarios were curated across 6 maneuvers, including vehicle following mined from leader-vehicle annotations.

## Making the replay faithful

The first replay drifted badly. Rebuilding it with time-synchronized PID control and road-aligned spawns cut tracking error from **5.9 m to under 0.3 m**.

## Screening for collisions before CARLA

An offline collision predictor screens about 5k scenarios in roughly 2 minutes and caught **20/20** failures in CARLA validation. Size-compensating trajectory separation then recovered **653** scenarios, for **2,912** collision-free scenarios in total.

## Before this: behavior cloning

The thesis grew out of my iHub-Data (IIIT Hyderabad) internship, where I trained a SegFormer-B1 behavior cloning model on ~66,000 image-command pairs (0.6876 m/s velocity RMSE, 4.89°/s steering RMSE), with TTC-based collision avoidance and PID controllers for closed-loop driving.

Next: a multi-modal imitation-learning policy trained on these scenarios. This line of work is also the basis of a paper under review at WAFR 2026.

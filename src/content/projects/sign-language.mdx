## The problem

Most sign language models recognize one sign at a time. Real signing is continuous: signs run into each other inside a sentence.

## How it works

1. **Landmarks, not pixels.** MediaPipe Holistic extracts 225 pose and hand landmark values from every frame, so the model sees how the body moves rather than the background.
2. **Fixed-length sequences.** Each video becomes a sequence of landmark frames of the same length.
3. **Sequence models.** I trained a BiLSTM and a Transformer to map each sequence to its sentence.

The data had 2,703 videos across 136 sentence classes.

## Results

| Model | Test accuracy |
| --- | --- |
| BiLSTM | 78.93% |
| **Transformer** | **89.09%** (BLEU 0.8593) |

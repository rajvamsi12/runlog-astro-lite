## The problem

Most self-driving research is trained and tested on structured, right-hand traffic: clear lanes, predictable merges. A junction in Gwalior has none of that. Auto-rickshaws cut across, bikes squeeze through gaps, cars overtake on either side.

A policy that has never seen this will fail on it. I saw that first-hand when a behavior cloning model I'd trained drove straight into a parked auto-rickshaw in simulation. To train and test on real Indian driving, I needed that driving inside a simulator.

## The idea

Record real traffic from a drone, track every vehicle, and replay those exact trajectories in CARLA. Then the simulator holds real behavior, and any driving policy can be dropped in next to it.

## How it works

1. **Map the real junction onto a CARLA map.** I split the junction into 6 regions (each approach road) plus the center, and calibrated each region separately onto Town10HD.
2. **Spawn matching vehicles.** Each real vehicle gets a CARLA blueprint of similar size, placed aligned with the road.
3. **Replay with physics.** A time-synchronized PID controller drives each CARLA vehicle along its real path.
4. **Catch collisions before simulating.** Small calibration errors can make vehicles overlap. An offline predictor screens about 5,000 scenarios in roughly 2 minutes and flags the ones that would collide.
5. **Repair what can be repaired.** Overlapping vehicles are nudged apart along their own paths, within fixed limits.

## Results

| | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate scenarios | 26,868 |
| Valid scenarios, across 6 maneuvers | 5,212 |
| Replay tracking error | 5.9 m -> under 0.3 m |
| Collision predictor, CARLA validation | caught 20 of 20 failures |
| Scenarios rescued by separating vehicles | 653 |
| Collision-free scenarios in total | 2,912 |

## What's next

A multi-modal imitation-learning driver trained on these scenarios is in progress. This line of work is also the basis of a paper under review at WAFR 2026.

## Where it started

Before the thesis, during my iHub-Data (IIIT Hyderabad) internship, I built the first version: spawn correction, time-to-collision based emergency braking, and a SegFormer-B1 behavior cloning model trained on about 66,000 image-command pairs (0.69 m/s speed error, 4.89°/s steering error).
